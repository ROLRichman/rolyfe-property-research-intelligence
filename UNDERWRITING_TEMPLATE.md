## Total cost
Total project cost = purchase price + all fees/charges + repairs + contingency + carrying/financing costs + exit costs.

## Maximum allowable offer (MAO)
Set a target return or equity cushion based on the intended exit. Subtract all non-purchase costs and required margin/reserves from the conservative exit value to estimate the maximum purchase amount. Do not use a generic formula without adapting it to the actual strategy and financing.
