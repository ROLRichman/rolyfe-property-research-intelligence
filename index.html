Property Map

0 mapped · 0 saved

Map starting. Save a property, then choose “Locate on map” on its saved record to add a pin.

Address pins are approximate geocoding matches, not surveyed locations, parcel outlines, ownership verification, comps, or live listing data. When you choose “Locate on map,” the property address is sent to OpenStreetMap’s public Nominatim search service and the returned coordinates are saved in this browser. No automatic geocoding or autocomplete runs in the background. See the Nominatim usage policy. This prototype uses public OpenStreetMap tiles with attribution; before broad commercial traffic, configure an appropriate tile provider or self-hosted service in accordance with its terms.
