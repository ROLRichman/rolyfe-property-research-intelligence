# Sale-Type Glossary — Research, Not Legal Advice

**Always use the county's official sale notice and governing law. Labels can vary by state and platform.**

| Sale label | What it usually means | Ownership timing | Key checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax lien / certificate | Buyer acquires a tax claim secured by property | Not immediate ownership | Redemption, interest rules, notices, foreclosure process |
| Upset sale (PA) | Tax claim sale under Pennsylvania's upset-sale process | Depends on completion and deed process | Surviving liens, taxes, notice, title review |
| Continued/adjourned upset sale | Upset sale rescheduled or continued | Depends on sale process | New date, updated list and terms |
| Judicial sale (PA) | Court-ordered tax sale process | Depends on court and deed process | Court order, lien effects, exceptions, title review |
| Tax deed / deed sale | Tax sale intended to convey a deed under local law | After statutory process | Redemption, deed timing, title defects, possession |
| Redeemable deed | Deed-related sale with a redemption right/period | May be delayed or defeasible | Redemption deadline and process |
| Mortgage foreclosure | Foreclosure of a mortgage/security interest | After auction and legal closing/confirmation requirements | Mortgage/court docket, other liens, occupancy, deposit |
| Sheriff sale | Sale conducted by sheriff under a court/legal process | Depends on jurisdiction and confirmation | Case docket, liens, title, possession and payment deadline |
| REO / bank-owned | Property owned by lender after foreclosure | Conventional conveyance process, subject to contract | As-is condition, title, closing timeline |
| Government surplus | Property disposed of by government entity | Per seller's terms | Conveyance restrictions, deposits, title and use |
| Special commissioner sale | Sale administered under a specific court or statutory process | Per sale order and local law | Governing order, notice, deposits and title |

## Critical distinction
A low opening bid is not proof of a bargain. It may exclude premiums, taxes, legal/title costs, repairs, occupancy costs, and surviving liens. Do not bid based only on an automated value estimate.
