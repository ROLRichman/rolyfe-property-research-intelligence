# Due Diligence Checklist

## Before bidding or offering
- [ ] Verify exact parcel and property type; avoid unit/address mismatches.
- [ ] Verify the sale is active and confirm the date, time, and current bid/price.
- [ ] Read the full official notice, terms, bidder affidavit, deposit, premium, payment and deed rules.
- [ ] Identify the exact sale type and redemption rules.
- [ ] Order/perform appropriate title and lien research; identify which liens may survive.
- [ ] Check taxes, municipal charges, HOA, utilities and other known obligations.
- [ ] Check occupancy, access, inspection rights, possession, and eviction requirements.
- [ ] Review county assessment and deed history.
- [ ] Verify zoning, permitted use, parcel boundaries, access, and easements.
- [ ] Check flood, environmental, structural, fire, roof, foundation, water, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, well/septic concerns as applicable.
- [ ] Obtain contractor estimates where inspection is permitted.
- [ ] Build conservative total-cost scenarios and compare against closed comps.
- [ ] Confirm available cash, deposit timing, funding conditions, and reserves.
- [ ] Obtain qualified legal/title advice when the sale process or lien effect is unclear.

## After winning or contracting
- [ ] Follow payment and filing deadlines exactly.
- [ ] Keep receipts, official notices, bid confirmation and correspondence.
- [ ] Confirm deed issuance and recording.
- [ ] Follow applicable redemption and notice rules.
- [ ] Do not enter, change locks, remove belongings, or represent possession rights until legally authorized.
- [ ] Reconcile final costs and update the deal file.
