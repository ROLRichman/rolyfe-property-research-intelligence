# Project Description

## Product name
**RO’Lyfe Property Research Intelligence Engine™**

## Tagline
**Find the Property. Verify the Facts. Calculate the Deal. Route the Capital.**

## GitHub short description
Multi-source property research, tax-sale and foreclosure tracking, parcel verification, due diligence, and conservative acquisition underwriting.

## What it does
A central research workspace for evaluating residential property opportunities across auction platforms, listing websites, public records, parcel-data providers, and partner workflows. It helps the researcher move from an unverified lead to a documented go/no-go decision.

## Who it serves
- Root Of Lyfe property research and acquisition workflow
- Family homebuyers exploring lower-cost properties
- Real-estate investors and acquisition partners
- Contractors estimating rehab needs
- Loan officers and funding partners reviewing a prepared deal package

## Main modules
1. Auction & County Watchlist
2. Property Intake / Address Normalization
3. Listing & Comparable-Sale Cross-Check
4. Parcel, Assessment & Tax Research
5. Sale-Type and Redemption-Risk Classification
6. Title, Lien, Occupancy & Access Checklist
7. Rehab Budget and ARV/MAO Calculator
8. All-In Cost and Exit Scenario Analysis
9. Evidence and Document Vault Index
10. Lender / Contractor / Buyer Handoff
11. Follow-Up and Campaign Readiness

## Guardrails
The engine must not represent estimates as verified values, guarantee a property is available, imply that a tax lien conveys immediate ownership, or treat an opening bid as the total acquisition cost. Legal and title conclusions require qualified review.
