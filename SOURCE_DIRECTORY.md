## Initial county research watchlist
Use official county sites to identify the county tax claim bureau, sheriff, recorder/register of deeds, assessor, court docket, and sale calendar. The following is a watchlist, not confirmation that each county uses GovEase or another named vendor:
- Pennsylvania: Monroe, Pike, Carbon, Wayne, Bucks, Philadelphia
- New Jersey: Union, Warren; expand by actual county sale schedule
- New York: Orange, Sullivan

## Evidence capture fields
For every source, store:
- URL
- Access/retrieval date
- What the source supports
- Whether it is official, marketplace, listing portal, estimate, or third-party lead
- Conflicts or missing information
- Next verification action
